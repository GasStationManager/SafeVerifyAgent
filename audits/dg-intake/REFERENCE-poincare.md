# Reference statement: the Poincaré conjecture

Written before reading the Lean statement, as a topologist would state the
theorem (Perelman 2002–03). One clause per list item; the statement tool
pairs each with the Lean clause it matches.

## Hypotheses

Let M be a topological space such that:

- H1. M is a topological 3-manifold: every point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to an open subset of ℝ³.
- H2. M is Hausdorff.
- H3. M is compact.
- H4. M is without boundary (a closed manifold is compact and boundaryless).
- H5. M is connected.
- H6. M is simply connected: its fundamental group is trivial.

## Conclusion

- C1. M is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere S³.
