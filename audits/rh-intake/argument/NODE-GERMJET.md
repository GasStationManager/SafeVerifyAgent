## 0. Verdict

**`germ_jet_zero` is correct and proved, and the proof is a recognisable classical argument.**

- **Reduction.** The analytic layer reduces the statement to a purely algebraic one.
- **The algebraic statement.** The degree-m symbol of the Schmid module, S^m𝔭⊗τ / S^{m−1}𝔭·(𝔭⊗τ)_−, contains no copy of τ for m ≥ 1.
- **How it is proved.** By the Hotta–Parthasarathy-type compact Casimir inequality. On the orthogonal complement of the relations, Cas_K ≥ c_Λ + 2m(g − 2n). This is strictly larger than the Casimir value c_Λ of τ.
- **Scope.** The result is uniqueness of *formal* jets at 1 of smooth bi-τ-equivariant solutions. Infinitesimal character, L² conditions and holonomicity play no role. The passage from jets to the function is one-variable analyticity along each ray t ↦ exp(tX).

**`mul_vminus_eq_zero` (Schmid's system for σ) is correct.** It is the standard Vogan-norm argument: the K-type Λ − β would have strictly smaller ‖·+2ρ_c‖. It uses only the minimality clause of `IsDS`.

**Nothing false-looking.** The parent's description "τ-multiplicity one in the Schmid module" is right. The Lean in fact proves the stronger graded form of it.

## 1. Statement

Lean (`FM/HighestWeightGapBound.lean:391`). Hypotheses, in mathematical language:

- **The representation σ.**
  - σ : G = U(J_ε) → B(H). `IsURepOn`.
  - V ⊂ H, with operators E_ab on V such that `IsDer` (`Definitions/SemilocalFrames/ActSingleNeZero.lean:12`) holds:
    - the E_ab satisfy the 𝔤𝔩_n relations (`GlRel`, `RPA/StatementA.lean:10`);
    - E_ab* = sgn(a,b)·E_ba (`IsUnitary`, StatementA:16);
    - d/dt σ(e^{tX})v at t = 0 equals Σ X_ab E_ab v for X ∈ 𝔤;
    - matrix coefficients are smooth.
  - So E is the complexified derivative action of 𝔤_ℂ = 𝔤𝔩_n on V.
- **The K-type τ.**
  - u ∈ V is a K-highest weight vector of weight Λ = `LamOf ε lam` = λ + ρ_n − ρ_c (StatementU:11, StatementA:30).
  - W = `kSpan ε E u` (the K-type τ generated by u) is finite-dimensional.
- **Gap.**
  - λ_a − λ_b ≥ g₀ for all a < b with ε a ≠ ε b, i.e. for all positive noncompact roots.
  - 2n < g₀.
  - The Harish-Chandra parameter condition is not needed here.
- **The function F.** F : M_n(ℂ) → End(H), and for g ∈ G and x ∈ W:
  - (i) F(g)x ∈ W;
  - (ii) F(k₁gk₂)x = σ(k₁)F(g)σ(k₂)x for k₁, k₂ ∈ K. So on W, F is a bi-τ-equivariant End(W)-valued function.
  - (iii) g ↦ ⟨y, F(g)x⟩ is smooth on G.
  - (iv) **Schmid's system** near 1: for every G ∈ `Vminus`, Σ_{a,b} E_ab⟨y, F(·)G_ab⟩ = 0 on a neighbourhood of 1 in G.
    - E_ab here is the complexified right-invariant derivative R(E_ab) (`Eab`, defined via `Rd` and `cpartMem`).
    - `Vminus` (StatementU:99) is the K-isotypic part of 𝔭⊗W = `arrV0 … 1` with highest weights Λ + (e_a − e_b) for b < a, ε a ≠ ε b, i.e. Λ − β with β positive noncompact.
    - Arrays G = (G_ab) are elements of 𝔤𝔩_n ⊗ W supported on noncompact (a,b). The K-action `arrE` is ad on the index plus τ on the value (StatementU:117-139; I checked that `factorAction` is the coefficient form of ad E_cd).
    - So (iv) says: R(D)F = 0, where D: 𝔭⊗W → W is restricted to (𝔭⊗W)_−. This is the 𝒟-operator.
  - (v) F(1) = 0 on W.

**Conclusion.** For every word w = (a₁b₁)…(a_kb_k), E_{a₁b₁}⋯E_{a_kb_k}⟨y, F(·)x⟩ evaluated at 1 is 0. That is, the full jet of F at 1, along all of 𝔤_ℂ, vanishes.

## 2. Argument

### Step A. Jets as a 𝔨-map out of the induced module (`FM/HighestWeightGapBound.lean:331-376, 391-486`)

- `exists_jet` packages the derivatives into J : W → (words → W), with ⟨y, J(x)(w)⟩ = E_w⟨y, F(·)x⟩(1).
- Because F is smooth and the E's are right derivatives, `eword_gl` (line 161) gives the 𝔤𝔩_n commutation relations: J(x)(w·ab·cd·w′) − J(x)(w·cd·ab·w′) = δ_bc J(x)(w·ad·w′) − δ_da J(x)(w·cb·w′).
- **Left equivariance** (k₂ = 1), via `eab_compact` and `eword_left`: J(x)((cd)::w) = E_cd J(x)(w) for compact (c,d). The outermost letter acts on the value.
- **Right equivariance** (k₁ = 1): J(E_cd x)(w) = J(x)(w ++ [cd]) for compact (c,d). The innermost letter is moved onto the argument.
- **Schmid:** Σ_ab J(G_ab)(w ++ [ab]) = 0.
- **Value at 1:** J(x)([]) = 0.

Mathematically, this says the jet is a map Φ ∈ Hom_𝔨(U(𝔤)⊗_{U(𝔨)}W, W):
- the right compact relation gives factorisation through ⊗_{U(𝔨)};
- the left compact relation gives 𝔨-equivariance for the left action, i.e. the diagonal action on S(𝔭)⊗W;
- Φ kills the 𝔤-submodule generated by (𝔭⊗W)_−;
- Φ vanishes on 1⊗W.

`jets_vanish` (line 244) realises this with V′ = the space of "jet functions" and the shift action (`shift`, `shift_gl`). It then calls the algebraic lemma.

**Bi-equivariance is handled exactly as the brief anticipated.** The two-sided K-action becomes: factorisation through U(𝔤)⊗_𝔨 W (right side), plus 𝔨-equivariance of Φ for the diagonal action (left side).

### Step B. The algebraic lemma (`FM/JetUniquenessAlgebraicStatement.lean`, `FM/JetUniquenessAlgebraic.lean:249-321`)

**`uniqAlg` statement.** Let V′ be any 𝔤𝔩_n-module. Let ι : W → V′ be 𝔨-equivariant with Σ E′_ab ι(G_ab) = 0 for G ∈ Vminus. Let ev : V′ → W be 𝔨-equivariant with ev∘ι = 0. Then ev vanishes on U(𝔤)ι(W).

**Proof.** Induction on the 𝔭-degree.
- `pfil k` is the span of noncompact words of length ≤ k applied to ι(W).
- `ew_mem_pfil`: any word of length k lands in pfil k, because compact letters preserve the filtration (`PfilFacts`).
- At degree j+1, consider T = ev∘θ∘ι on 𝔭^{⊗(j+1)}⊗W (θ = multiplication).
  - T is K-equivariant (`jm_theta_equiv`).
  - T is symmetric. Permuting factors changes θ by an element of pfil j (`thetaFil`), which is killed by the induction hypothesis.
  - T vanishes on arrays whose first slices all lie in (𝔭⊗W)_− (`theta_slices` plus the Schmid relation).
- Then `jet_map_eq_zero` gives T = 0.

**`jet_map_eq_zero`** (`FM/SymmetricKMapEqZeroOfVminusSlices.lean:114-227`). Statement: a linear map T : 𝔭^{⊗(j+1)}⊗W → W that is K-equivariant, symmetric, and zero on 𝔭^{⊗j}⊗(𝔭⊗W)_− (first slot) is zero.

Proof (read in full). Take f in 𝔭^{⊗(j+1)}⊗W orthogonal to ker T. Then:
1. **f is symmetric.** s·f − f ∈ ker T, and permutations are unitary.
2. **Every first slice of f lies in Vplus = (𝔭⊗W)_+** (highest weights Λ + β). If m is the Vminus component of a slice, then `sliceIns α′ m` ∈ ker T. Pairing with f gives ‖m‖² = 0. Here Vplus ⊥ Vminus and Vplus + Vminus = 𝔭⊗W, which is `typesSplit` (every K-highest vector of 𝔭⊗W has weight Λ + root).
   - So f ∈ `Nset` (StatementU:103).
3. **Casimir of f.** Cas_K f − c_Λ f ∈ ker T, because T(W-valued) is in W, where Cas_K = c_Λ = ‖Λ+ρ_c‖² − ‖ρ_c‖² (`kCasSpan`). Hence ⟨f, Cas_K f⟩ = c_Λ‖f‖².
4. **`nPos`** (`RPT/FirstSlotSliceSlotMixedCasimirZero.lean:517`, read). On Nset, Re⟨f, Cas_K f⟩ ≥ (c_Λ + 2(j+1)(g − 2n))‖f‖². Ingredients:
   - `CasArr`: Cas_K(arrays) = Cas_K(index slots) + c_Λ + 2Σ_i Ω_i. The index-slot Casimir is ≥ 0, since it is Σ XX* for compact X (I checked that `factorAction(c,d)`* = `factorAction(d,c)` in ℓ²).
   - `OmSymSlice`: for symmetric f, all ⟨f, Ω_i f⟩ are equal, and ⟨f, Ω_0 f⟩ is the sum over the slices.
   - `OmPlus` (`RPT/NoncompactArrFiniteDimensional.lean:433`, read): on the τ_{Λ+β} component, Ω_0 = (c_{Λ+β} − n − c_Λ)/2, where n is Cas_K on 𝔭 (`compact_casimir_positions_one`). This gives (c_{Λ+β} − n − c_Λ)/2 = ⟨Λ+ρ_c, β⟩ + 1 − n/2.
     - My check: for β = e_a − e_b, ⟨Λ+ρ_c, β⟩ = λ_a − λ_b + ρ_n(a) − ρ_n(b) ≥ g − (n−1).
     - So the value is ≥ g − 3n/2 + 2 ≥ g − 2n ✓.
5. **Conclusion.** Since g > 2n, c_Λ‖f‖² ≥ (c_Λ + positive)‖f‖², so f = 0. Hence ker T is everything.

**This is the precise algebraic lemma.** For m ≥ 1, Hom_K(S^m𝔭⊗τ / S^{m−1}𝔭·(𝔭⊗τ)_−, τ) = 0. The reason is that every K-type in the complement of the relations has compact Casimir > c_Λ.
- This is the Casimir comparison of Hotta–Parthasarathy / Schmid (‖Λ + Σβ + ρ_c‖ > ‖Λ + ρ_c‖), in its "symmetric tensor with + slices" form.
- **The gap (g > 2n) is used only in step 4.** It makes every Ω-eigenvalue on (𝔭⊗τ)_+ positive. It is sufficient, not sharp.

### Step C. From jet to function

`vanish_of_zero_jet` (`FM/IteratedDerivLine.lean:166`, statement and first lines read) works along a single ray:
- the t-derivatives of t ↦ f(exp tX) at 0 are words in the E's at 1, so they all vanish;
- the function is analytic in t on all of ℝ, so it is ≡ 0.

The analyticity of the difference comes from `lowK_analytic` (σ side) and `psiAnalytic` (candidate). No multivariable germ argument is needed. The Schmid system is used only near 1, and then only through jets at 1.

## 3. Test cases (c)

### U(1,1), τ = character
- 𝔭 = ⟨E₁₂, E₂₁⟩, and Vminus = ℂ·E₂₁⊗u. Schmid's system is R(E₂₁)F = 0.
- **Jet side.** τ occurs in S^m𝔭⊗τ only through (E₁₂E₂₁)^{m/2}. An element with every slot in Vplus = E₁₂⊗u has weight mβ ≠ 0. So τ ∉ Nset, matching the lemma.
- **Radial side.** This is the radial ODE f′ + m·tanh t·f = 0; with f(0) = 0 it forces f ≡ 0 ✓.

### U(2,1) middle series, ε = (T,F,T), τ = (λ₁,λ₃) ⊠ λ₂, N = λ₁ − λ₃
- **K-decomposition of 𝔭.** 𝔭 = P₊ ⊕ P₋ as K-modules: P₊ = ⟨E₁₂, E₃₂⟩ (highest weight e₁−e₂) and P₋ = ⟨E₂₃, E₂₁⟩ (highest weight e₂−e₃).
- **Vminus.** It is τ_{Λ−(e₂−e₃)} ⊂ P₊⊗τ together with τ_{Λ−(e₁−e₂)} ⊂ P₋⊗τ.
- **Degree 2.** τ occurs twice in S²𝔭⊗τ: once from the K-invariant in P₊P₋, and once from adjoint⊗τ when N ≥ 1. The relations P₋⊗τ_{Λ−(e₂−e₃)} and P₊⊗τ_{Λ−(e₁−e₂)} each contain τ once. The Casimir lemma says these two copies span the τ-isotypic part. This is consistent with the parent's second-derivative check.
- **Radial count.** a_t = exp t(E₁₂+E₂₁) and M = {diag(z,z,w)}.
  - τ|_M has N+1 distinct characters, so dim End_M(τ) = N+1 unknown radial functions.
  - The equations take values in Hom_M(τ, τ_−), which has dimension of order 2N.
  - **On t > 0 the radial system may have a solution space larger than one.** That does not contradict the lemma.
- **Why there is no contradiction.** The lemma concerns germs at 1 of *smooth* functions on G. Polar coordinates are singular at t = 0, and smoothness at 1 imposes the regularity conditions:
  - f_i(0) all equal, since F(1) ∈ End_K(τ) = ℂ;
  - plus the higher compatibility conditions.
- **The brief's worry about non-L² germs.** That worry is answered: formal smooth solutions at 1 are ≤ 1-dimensional for every λ with gap > 2n. Any analytic solution is a multiple of the true coefficient along every ray. L² is an extra global property that is never needed.
- **Not done:** an explicit rank computation of the U(2,1) radial system.

## 4. `mul_vminus_eq_zero` (`FM/HighestWeightDerivativeSumZero.lean:80`, read in full)

**Statement.** Assume:
- IsHCParam λ;
- IsDS ε λ σ;
- IsDer;
- u K-highest of weight Λ, with finite-dimensional K-span.

Then Σ_ab E_ab G_ab = 0 in V for every G ∈ Vminus. That is, multiplication 𝔭⊗τ → H kills (𝔭⊗τ)_−.

**Proof.**
- θ(G) = Σ E_ab G_ab is K-equivariant (`arr_module_facts`). So θ of a K-highest vector G of weight Λ − β is, if nonzero, a K-highest vector of weight Λ − β in σ.
- Its K-span is finite-dimensional, since it lies in pfil 1.
- So it is a K-type of σ (`kType_iff`).
- Now ‖Λ − β + 2ρ_c‖² = ‖u‖² − 2(u_a − u_b) + 2, with u = Λ + 2ρ_c = λ + ρ_std (`rhoN_add_rhoC`) and β = e_a − e_b, a < b.
- Also u_a − u_b = λ_a − λ_b + (b − a) > 1, because λ is strictly decreasing.
- So the Vogan norm strictly drops, contradicting the minimality clause of `IsDS` (`Formalism/SharpRestriction/CuspSpanNormBounds/RelevantZeroGramTerms.lean:72-76`).
- This is Vogan's standard argument, and it is correct.
- **What is used from `IsDS`:** only "Λ is a K-type of σ minimising ‖μ + 2ρ_c‖²". Square-integrability, irreducibility and infinitesimal character play no role here.

## 6. Oddities

1. **Gap threshold.** The gap needed is only > 2n. It enters only through the Ω-positivity on (𝔭⊗τ)_+, and is not sharp. Upstream it is 120·n·q^{3/2} (already noted by the parent).
2. **Stronger than needed.** The lemma is stronger than "multiplicity one of τ in the Schmid module". It is the graded/symbol version: τ does not occur in gr^m for m ≥ 1. It needs no infinitesimal character and no L² hypothesis.
3. **Hypothesis on F.** F is required only to be bi-equivariant and smooth, with the system holding near 1. Matrix coefficients outside W are irrelevant.
4. **`IsHCParam`.** It appears only in `mul_vminus_eq_zero` (strict decrease of λ), not in `germ_jet_zero`.
5. **Degenerate case.** `isDer_trivial` (E = 0) satisfies `IsDer`, so the hypotheses are not vacuous-looking. But a degenerate instance forces u = 0 or Λ = 0, which is harmless.
6. Nothing read is false as literally stated.
