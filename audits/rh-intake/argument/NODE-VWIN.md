## 1. Statement

The Lean says (`CB/ExistsPageN.lean:12-49`):

```lean
def VVle (E) [Field E] [NumberField E] (t : ℕ) (B : ℝ) : Prop :=
  ∀ Ω [Field Ω] [Algebra E Ω] [IsAlgClosure E Ω] (ld : ∀ p [Fact p.Prime], LData E Ω p),
    (∀ p [Fact p.Prime] (e : ℕ), 0 < e → e < t → shaLen (ld p) e ≠ ⊤) ∧
    ∀ S : Finset Nat.Primes, ∑ l ∈ S, log l * ∑ e ∈ Ico 1 t, (shaLen (ld l) e).toNat ≤ B

    a ≡ ±1 mod 2^(e+2) ∧ ψ(a) = 1 ∧ (a/q) = 1 ∧ (a/d) = 1)
structure WinPair eps Y q d : q prime, q ≡ 3 (8), Y^(ε/5)/2 < log q ≤ Y^(ε/5),

    ∃ J ≥ 1, ∃ X0, ∀ X ≥ X0, ∀ q d, WinPair ε (X^(1/J)) q d →
      ∃ Y, X^(1/J) ≤ Y ∧ Y² ≤ X ∧ ∀ x ∈ [Y, Y²], VAt ψ ε Ct q d (e x) x
```

Unfolded cohomology (`Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean:13-25, 50-80`;
`ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean:90-146`;
`ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/SeparatedDvrSecondCohomologyMem.lean:38-66`;
`ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/ContinuousConstSMulQuotient.lean:168`):
- `LData E Ω l`: S = the set of finite places of E above l (`hS`), a chosen place of Ω over each v ∈ S,
  and a character `chi : G_{E,S} → ℤ_l^×` whose pull-back to G_E is the l-adic cyclotomic character `cycZ`.
  `G_{E,S}` = `GS` = Gal(Ω/E) modulo the closure of the normal subgroup generated by the inertia groups at all
  finite places outside S. So it is the Galois group of the maximal extension unramified outside l (no condition
  at infinity).
- `QZ l` = ℚ_l/ℤ_l (discrete). `qzTw D e` = ℚ_l/ℤ_l with G_{E,S} acting through chi^{-e}, i.e. **ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−e)**.
- `H1` = continuous 1-cocycles modulo coboundaries. `Sha D e` = Sha1 = the kernel of restriction
  H¹(G_{E,S}, ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−e)) → ∏_{v|l} H¹(D_v, ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−e)). Only places above l are used; archimedean places are not.
- `shaLen` = ℤ_l-module length (ℕ∞), so it is finite iff the group is finite, and then log|Sha| = len·log l.

**In words.** Fix a primitive Dirichlet character ψ, ε ∈ (0,1/52) and any function e(x) with 2^{e(x)} ≈ x^{2+8ε}.
Then there is J ≥ 1 such that, for all large X and every "window pair" of primes (q,d) at scale X^{1/J}, there is
Y ∈ [X^{1/J}, X^{1/2}] such that for every x ∈ [Y, Y²] the following holds. Let E be the abelian field cut out of
ℚ(ζ_N) by {a : a ≡ ±1 mod 2^{e(x)+2}, ψ(a) = 1, (a/q) = (a/d) = 1}. Then:
1. for every prime l and every 1 ≤ k < ⌊x^{401}⌋, Ш¹(G_{E,S_l}, ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−k)) is finite, and
2. Σ_{l} Σ_{1≤k<⌊x^{401}⌋} log #Ш¹(G_{E,S_l}, ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−k)) ≤ exp(x^ε/2).

*I infer:* E is a CM field. Complex conjugation a = −1 is not in the fixing group because (−1/q) = −1 for q ≡ 3 mod 4,
and the proof uses this (`isCMField_of_isChiField`, `AV/CMFieldExponentialBounds/AdmissibleOfPageN.lean:108`). Its degree
is about 2^{e}·ord(ψ)·4, which is about x^{2+8ε}. Its conductor divides 2^{e+2}·ψ.N·q·d, so log cond ≈ Y^{ε/5}log²Y.

**Plausibility, as literally read** (all of this is my inference, not Lean):
- By Poitou–Tate, Ш¹(ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−k)) is dual to Ш²(ℤ_l(k+1)) ⊆ H²(O_E[1/l], ℤ_l(k+1)). That group is finite for
  k ≥ 1 (Soulé 1979), and it is the l-part of K_{2k}(O_E) by Quillen–Lichtenbaum (Rost–Voevodsky). So
  **finiteness is known**, and the sum in (2) is log of the order of a "wild-kernel" piece of ⊕_k K_{2k}(O_E).
- Expected size: log|K_{2k}| ≲ n·k·log(kN), which is polynomial in x. The bound exp(x^ε/2) is superpolynomial, so it
  leaves enormous room. Statement (2) is believable.
- The minus part (eigenvalue (−1)^{k+1} of complex conjugation) is controlled by generalized Bernoulli numbers, through
  the Iwasawa main conjecture for abelian fields (Mazur–Wiles) together with a Coates–Sinnott-type formula. The
  project's `minusBound` = 3n t² log(tN) + C·Σ_{l|o} l n² t² log²(tlN) (`AV/StmtFields.lean:69-71`) is the shape that
  route would give.
- The plus part (eigenvalue (−1)^k) is where Bernoulli numbers do not apply. There the order is tied to
  indices of cyclotomic (Deligne–Soulé) elements, or equivalently to higher regulators (ETNC for abelian fields:
  Burns–Greither, Flach). I do not know of a published uniform polynomial bound in the conductor. **The plus part is
  the non-standard content.**
- Vacuity. Every range is nonempty: ε ∈ (0,1/52), e(x) exists, the twist range 1..⌊x^401⌋−1 and Y ∈ [X^{1/J}, X^{1/2}]
  (J is chosen by the prover). `LData` is inhabited mathematically, since cycZ factors through G_{E,S_l}. Sha is not zero
  by definition. Two vacuity points remain. First, WinPairs are rare: they exist by Linnik-type estimates, since d is a
  prime ≡ 7 mod 16 in an AP of modulus ≈ e^{Y^{ε/5}} up to size e^{Y^{ε/5}log²Y}. If none existed, VWinAt would be
  vacuous for those X, but VWin is a hypothesis-side fact for the parent and is not vacuous in general. Second, the sum
  uses `.toNat`, which is 0 at ⊤, but clause 1 forces finiteness first. **No trivialisation seen.**

### The actual mechanism (`of_four_alt` → `vwin_of_counts` → `vwin_of_joint`)
`of_four_alt` gets `ThmBab K 401` from `thmBab_of_lemmaK` (Poitou–Tate, local finiteness/duality, **Ferrero–Washington**,
LemmaK) and `ThmBcFree` from `Odd.thmBcFree_of (ML.iv …)`. It then applies `vwin_of_counts`
(`AV/HeightAndVanishing/JointWinOfCounts.lean:30-33`). That combines ThmBab with
`ThmHV := thmHV (point (sumIn …) (sumOut …))` into `JointWin 401` (`AV/PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean:88-94,145`).
`vwinAt_of_joint` (`AV/CMFieldExponentialBounds/AdmissibleOfPageN.lean:41-129`) then proves VWinAt:
1. Split Ш by prime. For l = 2 the bound is `BaAt` = `VVat 2 E t (exp(x^{4ε/5}))`, from ThmBab. For odd l,
   complex conjugation acts on Ш with eigenvalues ±1, and length(Ш) = length(Ш⁺) + length(Ш⁻)
   (`shaLen_eq_add`, `AV/LengthEqAddEigen.lean:86-104`). `vvle_of_parts` (`:127`) recombines the pieces:
   B = B₂·log 2 + B₋ + B₊.
2. **Minus part.** ThmBc / ThmBcFree (`PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean:59-67`): for every CM compositum E,
   Σ_{l odd} log l Σ_{k<t} len Ш⁻ ≤ minusBound(C, o, n, cond E, t). This is polynomial in t, n and log cond.
3. **Plus part.** HVAt (`:75-77`): Σ_{l odd} log l Σ_k len Ш⁺ ≤ (cond(E)·t)^C + C·exp(x^{4ε/5}). It comes from ThmHV,
   which decomposes Ш⁺ into Dirichlet-character components Vlen(ψ') (`Chr.V'`,
   `AV/CharacterLevelBounds/ShaFamMono.lean:15-34`). These are components of Ш¹ of ℚ_l/ℤ_l(−k)⊗ψ'^{-1} over ℚ. The
   pairs (l,k,ψ') are sorted into the cases I, II, III, V, Z (`AV/HeightAndVanishing/DirichletPrimeToLPartMulLPowerPart.lean:208-219`,
   `Counts.lean:53-73`), and each case bounds Σ log l·len by (l·N·k)^C or (N t)^C.
4. Elementary analysis (`log_conductor_mul_height_le_of_pageN`, `error_terms_le_sqrt_exp_rpow`) checks that
   B₂ log 2 + minusBound + (cond·t)^C + C e^{x^{4ε/5}} ≤ √(exp(x^ε)), because log cond ≈ Y^{ε/5}log²Y ≪ x^ε.

### Where the plus-part bound comes from (CountI, the generic case)
`count_i_of_printed_bound_k_kato_automorphy` (`DTC/LinkedUnlE.lean:49-54`) = `U3.countI (U3.corGK (propGK_of_numberU3U_of_oneCharU NumberU3U OneCharU))`.
- **OneCharU** (`AV/ModularityArgument/LinkedUnramifiedChains/WeightInfinitesimalCharacters/AdmissibleUnitaryThreePoint.lean:116-127`).
  Take an imaginary quadratic K, a character ψ' satisfying the conditions GD (`Counts.lean:9-22`: l odd, l ∤ h_K·disc K,
  ψ' of parity matching k, l−1 ∤ 2k, …), σ ∈ G_K, and t = Ψ(σ) + 1 + Ψ(σ)^{-1}, where Ψ = χ_cyc^k ψ'. Suppose no
  "admissible" 3-dimensional ρ : G_K → GL_3(ℚ̄_l) has tr ρ(σ) = t. Then Vlen(ψ') < ∞, and
  Vn(ψ') ≤ 2·Σ_{ρ∈X} v_l(tr ρ(σ) − t) over a finite set X of pairwise non-isomorphic admissible ρ. "Admissible" means
  either `Adm3U` (irreducible, conjugate-self-dual automorphic after a twist, Hodge–Tate weights (k,0,−k), level N) or
  `Adm2` (endoscopic: W ⊕ 1 with W attached to a weight-(2k+1) newform) (`AV/AutomorphicForms/CongruenceNumberRankThree.lean:105-121`).
  So the Ш-component is bounded by the **congruences between the Eisenstein (reducible) representation Ψ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ^{-1}
  and cuspidal or endoscopic U(3) Galois representations**. *I infer* that OneCharU is proved by a
  pseudo-deformation / R^{red} = T argument. `AdmI` (`:129-135`) says that every ℚ̄_l-point of the deformation ring
  `D.Ring` that is of type I and automorphic gives an Adm3U representation. KatoUse (Bloch–Kato Selmer vanishing for
  newform twists, `AV/UnitaryThreeGroups/KatoUse.lean:13-17`) handles the endoscopic Adm2 contributions.
- **CongruenceNumberRankThree / NumberU3U** (`CongruenceNumberRankThree.lean:127-142`). For σ = Frob_w with w | p, p ≥ 7,
  there is a nonzero integer NN with log|NN| ≤ c·#Orb·(N(k+1))^A·log p, such that ‖NN‖_l ≤ ∏_ρ ‖tr ρ(σ^{-1}) − eis3‖_l
  for all l and every admissible family, and tr ρ ≠ eis3. *I infer*: NN is the norm of ∏_ρ(a_p(π) − Eisenstein
  eigenvalue) over the polynomially many U(3) automorphic forms of level N and weight k. The non-equality comes from purity.
- PropGK (`KatoUse.lean:36-44`) combines the two: Σ_{ψ'} Vn ≤ 2 v_l(NN). Summing over l with weight log l gives
  ≤ 2 log|NN|. CorGK (`:46-54`) uses two auxiliary primes p, p'. The result is a bound polynomial in N and k, which
  is the (h.N·t)^C of CountI.

### LemmaK (generic finiteness and size)
`LemmaK CK` (`AV/GeneratorCountTuples/LemmaK.lean:13-17`): for every totally complex L containing μ_l (μ_4 if l = 2) and
every k ≥ 1, H²(G_{L,S_l}, ℤ_l(k+1)) is finite with log|H²| ≤ exp((2kn)^{CK}·log(2|disc L|)). This is Soulé finiteness
with a crude explicit bound. It is too weak to give VWin by itself, since it is exponential in the degree n, but it is used
in the l = 2 and layer bounds (ThmBab) and inside the counts.

## 3. How Row0b is used
Row0b (h) enters as field `gal` of `LiteratureAutomorphyHypothesis` (`DTC/TopMainZj.lean:47`) and as the content of
`OpenImageCondition.mk h`. Its consumers are the many `P.litN.aut.gal K Ω l ι n π χ hreg hpol` calls. Examples:
`AV/AutomorphicForms/TemperedLocalComponents/ExistenceOfGaloisRepresentationsOfHyp.lean:50` (`galoisExistence_from_published`),
`…/WeightInfinitesimalCharacters/LevelKU.lean:72`, `AV/ModularityArgument/SemisimpleLevels/LevelKO.lean:106`,
`LevelPS.lean:64` and `TheoremsMuLinked/PurityTempered/Temper.lean:22`. In each case a regular algebraic polarized cuspidal π on GL_n
over a CM field K (here n = 3, K imaginary quadratic) is given its l-adic Galois representation with local-global
compatibility, Hodge–Tate weights and polarization. Those representations are the ρ in the sets X of OneCharU and
CongruenceNumberRankThree (Adm3U requires `IsAutCsd`), and purity (`Temper`) gives tr ρ ≠ Eisenstein trace. So Row0b
supplies the automorphic side of the Eisenstein-congruence bound, not an L-value comparison.

## 5. Oddities
1. **Hypothesis h is redundant.** `SmallClassNumbers/CorGKOf.lean:20-25` defines a closed term
   `litN : LiteratureRankNHypothesis` from `galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter`, and a closed `corGK`. VWin is therefore
   effectively a closed theorem, and the Row0b argument of `vwin_of_row` is decorative. This is consistent with
   NODE-ROOT oddity 2.
2. **Mechanism vs. the direction of the literature.** The plus-part bound is an *upper* bound on Selmer/Ш lengths from
   Eisenstein–cusp congruences on U(3) (OneCharU). It is uniform over all l, all k < x^{401} and all characters of
   conductor ≈ exp(Y^{ε/5}log²Y). The classical Ribet/Mazur–Wiles method gives the opposite (lower-bound) direction. The
   upper direction needs residually reducible R = T for U(3) at every such l, and that is not a standard citation at this
   generality. **This is the place for expert scrutiny.**
3. Ш here uses restriction only to the places above l. That is fine for odd l, and E is totally imaginary, so ignoring
   the archimedean places is harmless.
4. `VVle` sums over arbitrary finite S, so in effect it bounds the sum over all primes. That is consistent.
5. `PageN` gives a range for e(x), but `IsChiField` reuses the letter e for the 2-power exponent while `VVle` uses e for the
   twist. These are separate variables and there is no clash in Lean, but it is easy to misread.
6. The constant 401 is "rank bound 400 + 1" (NODE-ROOT §d). 1/52 bounds ε. The degree of E grows like x^{2+8ε}, while
   the bound is only exp(x^ε/2). The slack is huge, so the statement is not tight anywhere: all the difficulty is
   in the uniform plus-part bound.
7. LemmaK's explicit bound exp((2kn)^C log 2D) is stated as a literature fact. Soulé's theorem gives finiteness, and an
   explicit bound of this shape should be checked against the source.
