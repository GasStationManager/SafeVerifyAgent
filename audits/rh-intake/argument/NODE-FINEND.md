## 0. Answers in brief
- **Part 1, the bridge: closed (modulo two local inputs).** Every point of S = `D.Ring U g₁` is an `OrdPt`, and this holds over
  any complete local domain A, not just over finite DVRs. The reason is that every field point of the local ring U.R/It is
  upper-triangular in the fixed order (Ψ, 1, Ψ⁻¹), with exactly those characters on inertia. The flag then descends to A,
  with h ≡ 1 mod m_A, because the three inertial characters are residually distinct. "It allows points outside CondT" does
  not happen.
  - It is the single component through the split point. `rtBasic` (PHT/RtBasic.lean:14) says `primesAt0 = {It}`, It is prime,
    l ∉ It, and dim = 13.
  - Other orderings and other components are not on It. That does not matter here: the bridge needs only It ⊆ CondT.
  - No crystalline condition enters either `It` or `OrdPt`.
- **Part 2, finiteness: closed relative to the propagation theorem. It is not circular, and the parent's worry is misplaced.**
  - `FinEnd` quantifies only over complete **DVRs** A of characteristic l (`IsCD`, CDS/IsUnitOrIsUnitOfAddOne.lean:107-110).
    It does not quantify over all characteristic-l rings, so R/λ is not in scope.
  - What is derived is **finiteness of the weight-0 (torus-invariant) subalgebra** (`FinA`), not "R/λ finite". The derivation
    uses a curve criterion (`finCrit`) that correctly detects nilpotents mod ϖ through DVR points.
  - R/λ itself need not be finite. Reducible extension-class families are torus orbits; they have residual charpoly and are
    allowed.
  - `FinPt` (over the auxiliary CM field L) comes from `OffFin`, an output of the same propagation theorem
    (`components_good_rank_three`) that also gives automorphy. That is Skinner–Wiles-style pro-modularity plus Hida
    finiteness over Λ at a fixed weight.
- **Part 3, adjoint vanishing: open (core).**
  - `AdjointLiftingTheorem` is an NT-shaped statement whose hypotheses carry **no enormous-image hypothesis**. In its place
    are: irreducibility on a fixed subgroup G′, density of regular semisimple elements (`N3`), `Free`, `Par`, ordinary flags,
    and automorphy.
  - The characteristic-0 "dual Selmer is detected by finitely many regular semisimple σ" step (`regDet`) is proved by an
    elementary and correct-looking argument.
  - The integral Taylor–Wiles/patching step (`count`, `etOf`, `b1O`, `has_operator_statement`) is where NT need enormous image.
    I did not read it.
  - The theorem is claimed proved in the tree from `HypothesesH'`. No adjoint-vanishing theorem appears among the
    literature inputs.
  - The dependence order is AutEnd3Alt → AutBAlt → RigidityFirstCase → M4. There is no loop.
- **(d) Two-signed points:** `TwoPt` (U3/ConjStable.lean:94-95) means a local ℚ̄_l-point that is neither `Upper` nor
  `Lower` on G_K. The other points are handled as follows:
  - points that are both upper and lower (diagonal) must equal the base point (`PtHyp.fixed`);
  - one-signed (reducible, non-split) points need no rigidity, contribute nothing to Σ, and are handled by M1/M3;
  - type II points (W ⊕ 1) are two-signed and are handled by `RigiditySecondCase`.

## 1. Statements

### It (PHT/IsInertiaInfiniteCyclotomic.lean:93-133)
The Lean says:
- U.R is the local universal lift ring of r0|G_{ℚ_l}, with `GCond.noCondition`.
- `ordIdeal` is generated by two sets:
  - the coefficients of charpoly ρ(s) − ∏_a(X − ψ_a(s)), for s ∈ HL ∩ I (HL = ker η ∩ ker τ, where ψ_a = Ψ^{wt1 a} and
    wt1 = (1, 0, −1));
  - the entries of (ρ(s₀) − ψ₀(s₀))(ρ(s₁) − ψ₁(s₁))(ρ(s₂) − ψ₂(s₂)), for any s_a ∈ HL ∩ I. This product is in a fixed order.
- `IY` is the saturation by del = l·dd₀·dd₁. The dd's are genericity elements built from s₀ and a Frobenius element fr.
- `Icr` = rad IY.
- `It` = the intersection of the minimal primes of Icr that lie in the kernel of the O-point r0 (the characteristic-0 split lift
  Ψ ⊕ 1 ⊕ Ψ⁻¹).

### CondT and OrdPt (UnitaryThreeDeformations/AdjointDiagonalRepPreservesWeightPiece.lean:29-39; CDS/RatTorsionFree.lean:21-27)
- `CondT`: there is h ∈ GL₃(R) with h ≡ 1 mod m_R such that h⁻¹rh is upper-triangular on D_B ∩ ker η (`Upper`,
  U3/BaseRepGL3NormalisedOfPsiEqOne.lean:22: entries with wt a b < 0, i.e. a > b, vanish). Its diagonal on I_B ∩ ker η is
  exactly (Ψ, 1, Ψ⁻¹).
- So CondT is the **ordinary condition with the filtration required**, in a fixed order, with inertial characters fixed
  (cyc^eτ, 1, cyc^{−e}τ⁻¹). On the rest of D_B the diagonal is free. There is no crystalline or semistable clause.
- `OrdPt` = residually r̄ + continuous + normalised (at c, g₁) + `away` + CondT at B.
  - `away`: trivial on inertia at v ∤ l wherever η and Ψ are trivial.

### exists_ordPt_of_point (CDS/WeightZeroAlgebraLeEntryAlgebra.lean:316-343)
- **Statement.** Given `FinA`, any ℚ̄_l-point ξ of the entry algebra of S is, after conjugation by a torus element t, the
  image of an `OrdPt` over a finite DVR A ⊂ ℚ̄_l.
- **Proof.**
  1. `points_finite` uses FinA to extend ξ (twisted by t) to Ξ : S.R → ℚ̄_l with finite range.
  2. `exists_range` puts that range inside a finite DVR.
  3. `ordPt_of_gd` (CDS/TriangularOfReducibleRankThree.lean:190-233) does the real work. For **any** local O-hom f : S.R → A,
     with A a complete local domain, push f is an OrdPt:
     - the residual, normalised and `away` clauses come from the representing property of S;
     - for CondT, `Loc.lemma2c` (PHT/ValuationRingClearLastRowEntry.lean:219-245) gives a flag over a finite extension of
       Frac A;
     - `triangular_of_reducible_rank_three` (:88-143) then uses an inertia element x with Ψ(x)² − 1 a unit. It diagonalises
       r(x) over A by h ≡ 1 mod m (`exists_canon`). Because the diagonal values are distinct, the k-flag must be the
       h-flag (`go_up`), so CondT holds over A.
- **`lemma2c`.**
  - It starts from `lemma2b` (PHT/CommutingUpperTriangularDistinct.lean:113-160): at the characteristic-0 generic point of
    U.R/It, the flag exists. It is obtained by taking an inertia element s with distinct ψ_a(s), diagonalising ρ(s), and
    using the triple-product relation from `lemma2a`.
  - The flag is D-stable because ρ(g) intertwines ρ(s) and ρ(gsg⁻¹), which are upper-triangular with the same distinct
    diagonal (`commuting_upper_triangular_distinct`).
  - It then specialises to an arbitrary field point through a valuation ring. This is the Iwasawa-triangularisation /
    properness of the flag variety (`triangular_form_specialization`, :195-217).
- This is the standard Geraghty/Thorne argument. **So every A-point of Spec R, including characteristic-l DVR points and
  non-generic points, is CondT-ordinary in the given order.**

### FinEnd, FinPt, finPtOf
- `FinEnd` (CDS/RatTorsionFree.lean:29-32): there is a finite-index U ≤ G_ℚ such that for every A with `IsCD O A` (a complete
  DVR with finite residue field and local structure map) killed by m_O, and every OrdPt r over A,
  charpoly r(g) = charpoly r0(g) for g ∈ U ∩ ker η.
- `FinPt` (CDS/PropagationTheoremGL2.lean:74-80), over a frame F for L: for every characteristic-l IsCD A and every lift ρ that
  is residually diagonal with the inertial diagonal characters fixed (= 1 where Ψ = 1), charpoly ρ(g) = ∏(X − Ψ̄(g)^{w_i})
  for **all** g.
- `FinPtOf` (:82-84): `OffFin S → FinPt`.
  - `OffFin` (:50): there is I ⊂ B with `FinLam I` (m_B ⊆ rad(I + m_O + (ψ_{v,i}(g) − 1 : g inertial)), i.e. B/I is finite
    over Λ at a fixed weight; FrobeniusSmoothVectors/LeAdicClosure.lean:209-213) and I ≤ π(p) for every off-triangular
    prime p.
- `finPtOf` (CDS/FinPtOf.lean:183-260+):
  - if ker ξ is off-triangular, then I ≤ ker φ, so m_B maps to 0, so every weight-0 monomial is residual or 0, so
    charpoly = ∏(X − diagonal) (`charpoly_of_cyc`);
  - otherwise the point is triangular, and the diagonal characters are pinned by a class-group-type statement (`char_eq`).
- `OffFin` is produced by `components_good_rank_three` (CDS/PropagationTheoremGL3.lean:213-229) as M ⊓ I₃:
  - M is the modular ideal, and AllGood says every TI prime lies over V(M);
  - I₃ covers the TE/endoscopic primes (`l53`, from the GL₂ run).
  - `absMain` (CDS/AbsMain.lean:27-30) returns goodPt and finPt together.
- `finEnd_of_finPt` (DTC/GdEndOf.lean:84) descends from L to K.

### How FinEnd is used: finA_of_end (CDS/GfResZpow.lean:179-200)
1. The input is FinEnd applied to the OrdPt `ordPt_of_gd f` at characteristic-l DVR points f of S.
2. `diag_eq_and_triangular_of_charpoly_eq` (:139-176, via `constOfChar`) then gives two facts: the diagonal entries equal r0,
   and r is upper- or lower-triangular.
3. Feeding this into `finCrit` (CDS/FinCrit.lean:122-205) gives W.gr 0 finite over O, and then `gf_fin` gives the
   weight-zero subalgebra finite over O.
- `finCrit` works as follows. Each weight-0 generator b of m_{gr0} lies in rad(ϖ). If not, `npe_dim_one` gives a 1-dimensional
  prime p with b ∉ p. Normalising R/p gives a characteristic-l DVR point at which b ≠ 0, contradicting the hypothesis.
- This is correct commutative algebra. **It needs only domain points.** The nilpotent structure of R/λ is irrelevant: nilpotents
  lie in every prime, and finiteness of gr₀ only requires the weight-0 monomials to be nilpotent mod ϖ.

### AdjointLiftingTheorem (DTC/AutomorphicPointGalois.lean:35-39)
- **Setting:** a CM field L without ζ_l, `Large L c`, 2 ≤ n ≤ 4, a residual r with `V0ok`, B the universal ordinary
  determinant ring, and x a point.
- **Hypotheses on ρ** (`AutomorphicPointLocallyGeneric`, :18-28; definitions in TheoremsMuLinked/OrdZero.lean:14-20, 56-58):
  - continuous, x = det ρ, arithmetic, `IsAutCsdA` (automorphic), unramified on I₀, with ordinary flags `HasFlag` at all v | l;
  - `Free`: distinct ratios of the diagonal characters at some g;
  - `TypeI`: absolutely irreducible on the fixed subgroup F.G′;
  - `N3`: every open set meeting G′ contains σ ∈ G′ with separable charpoly;
  - `Par`: the weights are regular, i.e. ψ_i/ψ_j·cyc^{m_j−m_i} has finite order on inertia, with m strictly decreasing.
- **Conclusion:** `AdZero`, i.e. every polarized Bloch–Kato cocycle of ad ρ is a coboundary. That is
  H¹_f(L⁺, ad ρ) = 0 in the polarized form.
- **Proof chain:**
  1. `adjoint_lifting_of_cohomology hH` (DTC/AdjointLiftingOfCohomology.lean:31; DTC/ThFinal.lean:18) supplies the result.
  2. `adjointLiftingTheorem_of_locPrints_of_core` (DTC/AdLOf.lean:104+) reduces BK classes to ordinary classes, using Free/Par
     at v | l.
  3. That reduction feeds into `DenseThresholdMainCase` (OrdZero).
  4. OrdZero comes from `core_of_ord_vanishing_two_phi_all` (DTC/TCoreOf.lean:20-60), which builds `RegDet` by `regDet`.
  5. It then calls `ordinary_vanishing_two_of_printed_hida` (DTC/TOrdVan.lean:159-193). That step uses:
     - `nmAbs`;
     - `hida_x_of_printed_hida` (J = the Hida Hecke kernel, with x ∈ V(J));
     - `core_o_of_literal_automorphy_n` (DTC/TCoreO.lean:129-160: `count`, `etOf`, `b1O`, `has_operator_statement` with
       auxiliary primes q and eigenvalues sg).
- **`regDet`** (TheoremsMuLinked/GaloisRepresentationTypes/RegDet.lean:9-73).
  - Z¹(G, ad ρ ⊗ ε) is finite-dimensional, so finitely many functionals z ↦ tr(z(σ)ρ(σ)^k), with σ regular semisimple in G′,
    suffice (`finDet`).
  - `Det.detG` then shows that tr(z(g)ρ(g)) = 0 on G′ forces z to be a coboundary.
  - *(inferred)* The trace of the dual-number deformation is constant, so by absolute irreducibility on G′ it is trivial on G′.
    The remainder is inflated from the abelian quotient with the nontrivial twist ε, where H¹ = 0.
  - This is a correct characteristic-0 argument. **It is the characteristic-0 half of NT's use of enormous image.**
- **RigidityFirstCase** (DTC/RigEnd3.lean:28-33): at a local ℚ̄_l-point of S of type I that satisfies `IsAutIAlt`,
  BlochKatoSelmerVanishes(ad).
  - It is proved by `rigIUE_alt` (DTC/ERigIU.lean:80-150) together with `rigEnd3_alt` (DTC/AutUpA.lean:87).
  - The inputs are the OrdPt from `ordPt_of_gd`, `matIrr_open_gd` (TypeI implies irreducible on every open subgroup), `FreeAt`
    from automorphy, and the base change to L, where AdjointLiftingTheorem is applied.

## 3. Oddities
1. **The parent's reading of FinEnd is wrong in two ways.** FinEnd ranges over characteristic-l DVRs only. Its consumer
   (`finA_of_end`) yields finiteness of the weight-0 part, not of R/λ. R/λ is genuinely infinite when extension-class
   families exist, which is harmless here. The toy k[[X,Y]]/(X²Y) is not a test of FinEnd.
2. **FinPt over L asserts the residual charpoly for all g**, while FinEnd restricts to a finite-index U. The U is the
   descent from L (finEnd_of_finPt).
3. **No crystalline condition anywhere in It or OrdPt.** The `IsType`/`IsLCr`/`PtsCr` predicates in the It file are not used
   by It. AutEnd3Alt must therefore treat ordinary, possibly non-crystalline, points. With the sub of weight e above the
   quotient, those points are semistable *(inferred)*.
4. **The ordering is fixed by the product order in `ordIdeal`.** Points ordinary in another order are not on It and are never
   needed.
5. **AdjointLiftingTheorem drops enormous image.** Its replacement (TypeI on G′ + N3 + RegDet) suffices in characteristic 0.
   Whether the finite-level Taylor–Wiles count goes through without it is exactly child 1.
6. **There is no circularity.** AdjointLiftingTheorem is proved from `HypothesesH'` alone. FinEnd and AutEnd3Alt are joint
   outputs of the propagation theorem. RigidityFirstCase consumes AutBAlt.
